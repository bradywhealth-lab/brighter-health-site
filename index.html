Frequently Asked Questions

A quick look at what it’s like to work with Brighter Health Solutions and how I help you navigate health coverage choices.

Do you replace the public marketplace?
Not always — but often, I help clients find alternatives that better match their health, usage, and budget. My job is to show you clearly how different options compare so you can make an informed decision.
What does it cost to work with you?
There’s no additional fee for my advisory services. I’m compensated when I help you enroll in a plan, which means you get a dedicated advisor without an extra line item in your budget.
Can you help if I already have coverage?
Yes. Many clients come to me with existing coverage that feels too expensive or confusing. I can review what you have, outline alternatives, and help you decide whether to adjust or switch.
Will you still help me after I enroll?
Absolutely. I come with every policy I help you sign up for. That means you can call or message me for questions, life changes, or issues instead of waiting on hold with a call center.
