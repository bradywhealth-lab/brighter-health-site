Frequently Asked Questions

Do you replace the public marketplace?
Not always — but often, clients find alternatives that better match their health, usage, and budget. You’ll see the comparison clearly so you can decide confidently.

There’s no additional fee for advisory services. Compensation happens when Brady helps you enroll, so you get a dedicated advisor without an extra line item.

Yes. Many clients arrive with existing coverage that feels too expensive or confusing. Brady can review what you have, outline alternatives, and help you adjust or switch.

Absolutely. Brady comes with every policy. Call or message for questions, life changes, or claims — no call-center maze.
